Read and memorize the Quran on its printed page

Safha is a free Android app with all 604 pages of the Madinah Mushaf offline, a Hifz mode that brings the words back as you recite, and a hadith library that names the scholar behind every grade.

Everything for your daily recitation

Built around the Mushaf you already know, so your memory of the page works for you.

The full Madinah Mushaf

All 604 pages with every line where it is printed, on four papers: Classic Madinah, Night, Parchment and Minimal. Reading works offline.

Hifz mode

The words are hidden and every ayah marker stays in place. As you recite, Safha brings back the words it hears, in order.

Hadith library

Every grade shows the scholar who gave it, never a grade made up by software, and each chain is traced narrator by narrator.

Tajweed in colour

Ghunnah, ikhfa, idgham, iqlab, qalqalah and the madd lengths, each in its own shade on exactly the letters it covers.

Learn Quranic Arabic

Lessons from the letters to Quranic grammar, levels of the Quran's most frequent words, and a root explorer for every word.

Qibla and three languages

A Qibla compass for prayer time, and the whole interface in English, Urdu and Arabic.

Every grade names its scholar

The Library shows each hadith with the grades scholars gave it and follows the chain to Ibn Hajar's rulings in Taqrib al-Tahdhib. Here is one hadith as the app shows it.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi 4 The Book on Purification
مِفْتَاحُ الْجَنَّةِ الصَّلاَةُ وَمِفْتَاحُ الصَّلاَةِ الْوُضُوءُ

“The key to Paradise is Salat, and the key to Salat is Wudu.”

Safha means page

صفحة is Arabic for page. Every page of the Madinah Mushaf keeps its printed lines in the app, so the page you memorized is the page you see, and each word comes back exactly where it belongs.
