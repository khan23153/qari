How this review works

Each clip is one word from a recitation. You judge it in two steps.

  1. Stage A: write what you hear. Listen to the word (and the whole recording if you need context) and write it with full harakat, exactly as pronounced, including any mistake. The Quranic text is hidden on purpose. Once you save Stage A it cannot be changed.
  2. Stage B: compare with the Mushaf. The reference word appears. Say whether the recitation matches it. If not, tap the letter where it differs and record what was expected, what was heard, and whether it is an acceptable way of reciting.

If you can't tell, say so. "Can't tell" and "audio not usable" are useful answers; please don't guess. Other reviewers' answers and any computer predictions are never shown here.
